# 🐍 Neon Snake Grid

**Capture territory. Battle neon trails. Don't die.**

A fast, mobile-first territory-capture arcade game. Steer your neon snake across a glowing grid, draw a trail, and loop back to claim the enclosed land as your own. Outmaneuver rival snakes, cut their trails to take them down, and grab the biggest slice of the board before the clock runs out.

**[xniperbuilds.github.io/neon-snake-grid](https://xniperbuilds.github.io/neon-snake-grid/)**

Best on a phone — swipe to steer.

## 🎮 How to play

- **Swipe** (or arrow keys) to change direction — you can't reverse into yourself.
- Leave your home zone to draw a **trail**, then return to your territory to **capture** everything you looped.
- Run into a rival's trail to **take them out** (and bank a bonus).
- Hit your **own trail** or a **wall** and it's game over.
- Claim the most territory to top the board.

## ✨ Features

- 4 modes — **Classic** (5 min), **Blitz** (3 min, faster, more rivals), **Endless**, and **Solo Practice**
- Smart rival snakes with adjustable difficulty (Easy / Medium / Hard)
- Live grid rendering with smooth camera, minimap, and capture animations
- Real-time global **leaderboard** (Today / This Week / All Time)
- 8 unlockable achievements + lifetime stats and player tiers
- Synthesized sound effects and pulsing background audio
- Shareable score card
- Works fully offline after first load (leaderboard needs a connection)

## 🛠️ Tech

Single self-contained `index.html` — HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio API, and a realtime backend for the leaderboard. No build step, no dependencies to install.
